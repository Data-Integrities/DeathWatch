ObitNOTE Retail Pricing Plan Version: Retail Price Table 1 Purpose:
Establish a simple, scalable pricing structure for individual retail
customers monitoring obituary publications for people they care about.

  ----------------------
  1. PRICING STRUCTURE
  ----------------------

Retail pricing is based on the number of people monitored. Plans are
billed annually.

Plan People Monitored Annual Price Cost per Person Tier 1 Up to 10
people $20 $2.00 Tier 2 Up to 25 people $39 $1.56 Tier 3 Up to 50 people
$69 $1.38 Tier 4 Up to 100 people $119 $1.19

Prices decrease per person as the monitoring list grows, encouraging
upgrades while maintaining strong margins.

  -----------------------------------
  2. EXPECTED CUSTOMER DISTRIBUTION
  -----------------------------------

Initial modeling assumes the following approximate distribution of
retail users:

Plan Estimated % of Customers 10 people ~55% 25 people ~30% 50 people
~10% 100 people ~5%

  -----------------------------
  3. DERIVED ECONOMIC METRICS
  -----------------------------

Based on the expected distribution:

Average revenue per customer (ARPU): ~$35.55/year Average people

These figures provide the baseline for evaluating:

-   Marketing acquisition cost (CAC)
-   Search infrastructure costs
-   Email notification costs
-   Support costs

  ----------------------
  4. GRACE SLOT POLICY
  ----------------------

To reduce user friction and improve fairness, each plan includes one
temporary grace slot.

Plan Normal Limit Grace Limit 10 people 10 11 25 people 25 26 50 people
50 51 100 people 100 101

Behavior:

If a user attempts to add one additional person beyond their limit: -
The system allows the addition. - The user is notified that they are
using a grace slot. - The user is prompted to upgrade.

If the user attempts to add another person beyond the grace slot, they
must upgrade before continuing.

  ---------------------------------
  5. UPGRADE AND DOWNGRADE POLICY
  ---------------------------------

Upgrades: Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the
remaining time in the current subscription period.

Example: Customer upgrades from: 10-person plan ($20/year) to 25-person
plan ($39/year)

If six months remain:

Difference per year = $19 Prorated charge = $19 × (6/12) = $9.50

The renewal date remains unchanged.

Downgrades: Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date. No partial
refunds are issued during the active subscription period.

  -----------------------
  6. PRICING PHILOSOPHY
  -----------------------

The ObitNOTE retail pricing model follows several guiding principles:

Simplicity: Customers understand pricing immediately without complicated
per-search fees.

Low barrier to entry: The $20 entry plan makes it easy for new customers
to try the service.

Natural expansion: Users often remember additional people later, leading
to organic upgrades.

Fairness: The grace slot prevents users from feeling blocked by strict
limits.

Alignment with use case: Retail users typically monitor: - former
classmates - old friends - former coworkers - neighbors - teachers -
coaches - distant relatives

Typical lists naturally grow to 15–25 people, making the pricing tiers
appropriate for real-world usage.

  -----------------------------
  7. CUSTOMER-FACING LANGUAGE
  -----------------------------

For retail customers, pricing should refer to people, not names.

Example:

Monitor up to Price 10 people $20/year 25 people $39/year 50 people
$69/year 100 people $119/year

Using “people” reflects the user’s intent and keeps the service
respectful and intuitive.
