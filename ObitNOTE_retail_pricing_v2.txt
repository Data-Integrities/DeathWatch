ObitNOTE Retail Pricing Plan Version: Retail Price Table 1 (v2 Internal
Specification)

Purpose Establish a clear, fair, and scalable retail pricing structure
for ObitNOTE customers who wish to monitor obituary publications for
people they care about.

This document defines the pricing tiers, upgrade rules, grace-slot
behavior, and billing logic required for implementation.

  ----------------------
  1. PRICING STRUCTURE
  ----------------------

Retail pricing is based on the number of people monitored. All plans are
billed annually.

Tier People Monitored Annual Price Cost per Person Tier 1 Up to 10
people $20 $2.00 Tier 2 Up to 25 people $39 $1.56 Tier 3 Up to 50 people
$69 $1.38 Tier 4 Up to 100 people $119 $1.19

Prices decrease per person as the list size increases, encouraging
natural upgrades while maintaining healthy margins.

  ----------------------
  4. GRACE SLOT POLICY
  ----------------------

Each plan includes one temporary grace slot beyond the official limit.

Plan Size Normal Limit Grace Limit 10 people 10 11 25 people 25 26 50
people 50 51 100 people 100 101

Behavior Rules

1.  The system allows the first person added beyond the limit.
2.  The user is notified they are using a grace slot.
3.  The user is encouraged to upgrade their plan.

Example message:

“You have added one extra person beyond your plan. Upgrade to monitor
more people.”

If the user attempts to add another person beyond the grace slot, an
upgrade is required before continuing.

  -------------------
  5. UPGRADE POLICY
  -------------------

Upgrades take effect immediately.

Charges are prorated based on the remaining time in the subscription
period.

Example:

Current Plan: 10 people ($20/year) Upgrade Plan: 25 people ($39/year)

Price difference: $19/year

If 6 months remain:

Prorated charge = $19 × (6 / 12) = $9.50

The original renewal date does not change.

  ---------------------
  6. DOWNGRADE POLICY
  ---------------------

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

No partial refunds are issued during the active subscription term.

This simplifies billing and prevents refund abuse.

  --------------------------------
  7. PRODUCT LANGUAGE GUIDELINES
  --------------------------------

Customer-facing language should refer to “people”, not “names”.

Correct: “Monitor up to 25 people”

Avoid: “Monitor 25 names”

This wording better reflects the user’s mental model and maintains a
respectful tone.

  --------------------
  8. TARGET USE CASE
  --------------------

Typical ObitNOTE customers monitor individuals who were once part of
their lives but are no longer in their daily information network.

Examples include:

• Former classmates • Old friends • Former coworkers • Neighbors •
Teachers • Coaches • Mentors • Distant relatives

Typical monitored lists grow naturally to 15–25 people over time.
